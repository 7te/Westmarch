## Contact details — strongly discouraged, your decision

This template deliberately does not prompt for phone, email,
address, date of birth, or account IDs. Those details are strongly
discouraged in a note vault, because anything in the vault should
be treated as if it could become public — see Sensitive
information in the README.

If you explicitly decide to add such details anyway, that is your
decision, and your responsibility, not the template's. Prefer a
separate local-only / encrypted store that is never pushed, synced
to cloud, or handed to an AI. Do not push personal contact details
into a public repo.
